Privacy Policy

Shop the Look · Effective date: August 20, 2026

Shop the Look ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

  • Photos you submit. When you analyze an outfit, the image is sent to our servers and third-party AI services (Google Gemini, Google Lens, fal.ai) for clothing detection and product matching. We do not store your photos after processing is complete.
  • Purchase & subscription data. If you subscribe to a premium plan, payment is handled by Apple (In-App Purchase) and RevenueCat. We receive a pseudonymous user ID and subscription status — not your payment details.
  • An anonymous installation ID. We generate a random identifier that is stored only on your device and used to understand how the app is used. It is not your device ID, not an advertising identifier, and is never used to follow you across other apps or websites.
  • Usage data. Which features you use and where the app succeeds or fails — for example that a scan completed, how long it took, and the general category of clothing detected (such as "Shoes"). We never record your photos, the specific products shown to you, prices, or the sites you visit.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To analyze outfit photos and return product matches.
  • To manage your subscription and unlock premium features.
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google (Gemini AI, Google Lens) — image analysis
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • PostHog — product analytics. Our PostHog data is hosted in the European Union.
  • fal.ai — AI image processing
  • Apple — In-App Purchases, App Store

4. Your Choice

You can turn off usage analytics at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy → Share usage data. The app works exactly the same with it off. Deleting the app also stops all collection.

5. Data Retention

Photos are processed in real-time and not stored beyond the analysis session. Subscription data is retained as long as your account is active. Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized.

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete the app at any time to stop all data collection.
  • Request deletion of any stored data by contacting us.
  • If you are in the EU/UK, you have rights under GDPR including access, rectification, and erasure.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
